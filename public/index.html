The Swamp Field Guide

Performance metrics and what they mean can get swampy, confusing, and gross. This tool helps evaluate any metric you enter.

If you enter Views + Engagements, we calculate ER automatically. If you enter ER only, we compare it to channel goals.

Views + Engagement benchmarks are based on averages (tiered: below / on-par / above). Insights are directional prompts — not final creative diagnoses.

  • Big views, low engagement? Hook hit, but bite missed.
  • Low views, high engagement? Small crowd, strong love.
  • Above goal ER? Release the jibbitz.
